# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility,
and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
personal or political attacks, trolling, insulting comments, and publishing private information
without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through the contact information
on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily
or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate or harmful.
